# HighJump local gameplay test — 2026-10-30

I ran Anomoly against a local vanilla Minecraft Java 26.3 server. A stone block at (80, 120, 80) held the player at feet y=121. I disabled other movement modules, assigned `B` to HighJump, pressed Space for each jump, and queried the server-side `Pos[1]`. The module launches from the normal `LivingEntity.jumpFromGround()` path. Its motion setting defaults to 0.8, matching the [pinned LiquidBounce source](https://github.com/CCBlueX/LiquidBounce/blob/2b0edfcf2/src/main/kotlin/net/ccbluex/liquidbounce/features/module/modules/movement/highjump/ModuleHighJump.kt); the [publisher documentation](https://liquidbounce.net/docs/modules/movement/highjump) lists 0.44.

| Configuration | Server Y samples after jump |
| --- | --- |
| Disabled, ordinary jump | 122.25, 121.00 |
| Vanilla mode, motion 0.8 | 124.42, 123.90, 121.00 |
| Vulcan mode, motion 1.2, glide on | 126.00, 128.89, 128.23, 126.85, 125.31, 124.00, 122.61, 121.30, 121.00 |
| Vulcan mode, motion 1.2, glide off | 127.32, 129.19, 126.62, 121.84, 121.00 |

The vanilla mode rose higher than the ordinary jump. The nondefault motion value produced a stronger launch. With the same 1.2 motion in Vulcan mode, glide extended the measured descent relative to glide off. Samples were taken through server console queries at roughly 0.3 to 0.5 second intervals; the numbers are observations rather than exact apices or a synchronized timing study.

These tests establish movement behavior on a vanilla server. They do not establish that Vulcan mode evades any anticheat, avoids flags, or prevents kicks and bans. The pinned upstream source itself [notes that its Vulcan mode still flags](https://github.com/CCBlueX/LiquidBounce/blob/2b0edfcf2/src/main/kotlin/net/ccbluex/liquidbounce/features/module/modules/movement/highjump/ModuleHighJump.kt#L467-L476). Higher motion values, other movement modules, and third-party anticheats remain untested.
