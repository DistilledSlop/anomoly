# Self Trap gameplay test — 2026-10-30

Tested with Anomoly on a local vanilla Minecraft Java 26.3 server. PlayerA stood at `(20.5,100,20.5)` on a flat stone floor. The surrounding space was cleared from Y 100 through Y 103, so there was no existing ceiling or side support. PlayerA was in creative mode with stone selected in hotbar slot 0, 16 obsidian in slot 1, and an empty offhand. Self Trap used its default line-of-sight and place-range checks and normal `MultiPlayerGameMode.useItemOn` block interactions.

| Probe | Server observation |
| --- | --- |
| Build from bare floor | Obsidian appeared at `(20,100,19)`, `(20,101,19)`, `(20,102,19)`, and the roof at `(20,102,20)`. The selected item was still stone. |
| Repair from offhand | After clearing hotbar slot 1, putting obsidian in the offhand, and removing the roof, the roof became obsidian again. Stone remained selected. |
| No material | With both the offhand and hotbar obsidian removed, clearing the roof left it as air. |

No kick or disconnect occurred. This verifies the default obsidian support-and-roof path and offhand repair on a vanilla server. Alternate configured blocks, moving players, protected regions, and third-party anticheat compatibility remain unverified.
