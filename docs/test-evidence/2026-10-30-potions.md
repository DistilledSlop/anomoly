# Healing potion gameplay tests — 2026-10-30

I ran the 26.3 Fabric client against a local vanilla 26.3 multiplayer server on a level stone platform. Potions were standard `minecraft:splash_potion[potion_contents="minecraft:healing"]`, added through server commands. The server reported health with `data get entity <player> Health` and counted remaining potions with `clear <player> minecraft:splash_potion 0`, which does not remove them. Stone began in the selected hotbar slot.

## AutoPotion and AutoPot

With AutoPotion enabled at its 12-health threshold, I damaged Player87 from full health to **8.0**. Without manual use input, the module selected and threw one healing splash potion at the player's feet. The server then reported **16.5** health and five potions remaining from six. The selected item was again stone. This one module implements both catalog entries, AutoPotion and AutoPot.

For a negative control, I replaced the inventory with three splash potions of harming and brought health below the threshold. After waiting, all three remained. The selector requires an instant-health effect and rejects potions carrying harmful effects.

## ThrowPot

I disabled AutoPotion in the ImGui ClickGUI, gave Player60 four healing splash potions, and lowered health to about **6.5**. Enabling ThrowPot in the ClickGUI and closing it consumed **two** potions; the server reported **14.5** health and stone selected. The module switched itself off after the sequence.

With health above ThrowPot's 8-health two-potion threshold, I toggled it again. The server counted **one** additional potion consumed and health rose to **18.5**. The [captured game frame](2026-10-30-throwpot.png) shows the stone slot selected and one potion remaining after that run.

Both modules use the normal item-use packet with a downward pitch and restore the player's view immediately afterward. Potion splash strength depends on where the bottle breaks, so exact healing can vary. These tests used a local server without an anticheat plugin; they do not establish anticheat compatibility.
