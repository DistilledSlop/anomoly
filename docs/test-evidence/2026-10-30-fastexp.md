# FastExp local gameplay test — 2026-10-30

I ran Anomoly in a local vanilla Minecraft Java 26.3 multiplayer world. FastExp was enabled and its separate hold key was bound to V; Auto Exp was disabled. Server commands equipped items and queried damage, bottle counts, and the selected item after actual client ticks. Normal mode used its default two-to-three tick interval and NoWaste was enabled unless noted.

| Case | Server observation |
| --- | --- |
| Normal Mending repair | A Mending iron helmet at damage 120, stone selected, and 32 hotbar bottles: holding V used seven bottles and reduced damage to 6. Stone returned to the selected slot. |
| NoWaste stop | Holding V again for three seconds left the helmet at damage 6 and 25 bottles untouched. |
| No-Mending control | Replacing the helmet with a damage-120 iron helmet without Mending, then holding V for three seconds, left all 25 bottles untouched. |
| Offhand held-tool repair | A Mending iron pickaxe at damage 200 stayed selected while offhand bottles fell from 16 to 4 and the pickaxe damage fell to 18. The hotbar bottle stack remained at 25. |
| Fast mode without NoWaste | With stone selected and 64 hotbar bottles, a roughly 0.6-second hold used 51 bottles. Stone was selected afterward. The local server did not kick the player. |
| Fast mode with NoWaste | A Mending helmet at damage 120, stone selected, and 64 bottles: a four-second hold used nine bottles and reduced damage to 2. A second hold used no more bottles; stone stayed selected. |
| Disconnect cleanup | A server kick changed FastExp's saved module state from enabled to disabled. |

NoWaste begins only when an eligible Mending item reaches the configured remaining-durability threshold and continues until the configured durability percentage is exceeded. It estimates outstanding repair from thrown bottles to avoid a large burst while XP orbs are still in flight. The exact combat-pause and slot-reset-delay timing, custom Mending behavior, and third-party anticheats were not separately tested.
