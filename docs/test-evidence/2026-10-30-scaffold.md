# Scaffold and Safe Walk gameplay test — 2026-10-30

Tested with Anomoly on a local vanilla Minecraft Java 26.3 server. A fresh survival player, ScaffoldTester, started on a stone ledge ending at x=202, y=99, z=200, facing east with Auto Walk enabled. The y=99 cells from x=203 onward were air; a stone safety floor was nine blocks lower. Server console queries measured player position, blocks, inventory, and selected slot.

| Probe | Server observation |
| --- | --- |
| No building blocks | With Scaffold and its default edge guard enabled, the player stopped at `(203.285810,100,200.5)` and stayed there. The first gap cell `(203,99,200)` remained air. |
| Hotbar bridge | Supplying 16 stone in hotbar slot 1 while a wooden sword stayed selected in slot 0 produced a continuous stone bridge from x=203 through x=218 at y=99, z=200. All 16 stone were consumed, the sword remained selected, and the player stopped at `(219.291099,100,200.5)` at the new edge. |
| Offhand continuation | Supplying three stone in the offhand extended the bridge through x=221. The offhand stone was consumed, the sword remained selected, and the player stopped at `(222.294844,100,200.5)`. |
| Falling-block filter | With five sand in hotbar slot 1 and no other building blocks, x=222 stayed air, all five sand remained, and the player stayed at `(222.294844,100,200.5)`. |
| Safe Walk alone | After disabling Scaffold and enabling Safe Walk, the same player resumed at `(222.294844,100,200.5)` with Auto Walk enabled. It remained at that position and y=100 while x=222, y=99 stayed air. |
| Optional tower mode | With Auto Walk off, `auto_tower=true`, a sword selected, and four stone in hotbar slot 1, Scaffold jumped in place at `(240.5,200.5)` and built a four-block column at y=100 through y=103. The player landed at y=104, all stone was consumed, the sword remained selected, and y=104 stayed air after material ran out. |

The first Scaffold attempt exposed a registration defect: `PlayerSafeWalkMixin` existed in the source but was absent from `anomoly.mixins.json`. Without that mixin, the player walked off the ledge and fell to the safety floor. The registered-mixin run above is the verified result. Scaffold uses ordinary block-use interactions and excludes falling blocks, block entities, and blocks without a full collision cube. Its optional visible-face check was not tested; the default allows placement at an otherwise unreachable support face and may be rejected by a third-party anticheat. Sprinting, diagonal movement, moving jumps, offhand tower material, and protected regions need separate gameplay tests.
