# Auto Farm gameplay test — 2026-10-30

Tested with Anomoly on a local vanilla Minecraft Java 26.3 server. A survival player stood near prepared plots with an iron pickaxe selected and planting items in the hotbar. Server console queries checked block states, inventory, and selected slot. Auto Farm used normal mining and block interactions.

| Probe | Server observation |
| --- | --- |
| Mature field crops | Wheat, carrots, potatoes, and beetroot at their maximum ages were harvested and returned as nonmature planted crops on their farmland. Carrots and potatoes were directly observed at age 0. Mature nether wart was likewise harvested and replanted on soul sand. None of the five remained mature. The selected slot returned to 0. |
| Sweet berries | A bush at age 3 was picked through a normal block interaction and became age 1 without losing the bush. |
| Melon and pumpkin | Both fruit blocks were mined to air. Their growing stems were not part of the test setup. |
| Sugar cane | With a stable dirt base beside water, the upper cane was mined while the lower cane stayed in place. An initial falling-sand support setup failed because the sand had no block beneath it; the corrected support was used for the result. |
| Cactus and bamboo | The upper segment of each was mined while the base segment remained. Cactus stood on sand supported from below; bamboo stood on dirt. |
| Kelp | A three-block water column was cut above its base. The bottom kelp remained and became the new head; the two cells above returned to water. |
| Cocoa | Mature cocoa on a jungle log was mined and replanted on the same log face at age 0. Cocoa beans fell from eight to seven. This rerun followed a fix to preserve the planting intent while the mined block update was in flight. |
| Bone meal | With optional bone meal enabled, one bone meal was consumed and wheat advanced from age 0 to age 5. |
| Empty plot | An empty farmland cell was planted with the preferred wheat seed. |
| Line of sight | A mature wheat crop behind a stone wall stayed at age 7. Removing the wall let Auto Farm harvest and replant it at age 0. |

Range, vertical scan height, action interval, preferred empty-plot crop, column and fruit harvesting, berry picking, replanting, bone meal, line of sight, facing, tool selection, and hotbar restoration are configurable. Bone meal is off by default. Protected-region behavior, moving players, mixed crop inventories beyond the tested field, and third-party anticheats need separate tests.
