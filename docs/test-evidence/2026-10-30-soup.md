# Auto Soup and Soup gameplay test — 2026-10-30

Tested on a local vanilla Minecraft Java 26.3 multiplayer server with a Fabric client. The player was in survival mode. Hunger was lowered with the server's Hunger effect so vanilla stew could be consumed. The client had stone selected in hotbar slot 0. No mouse use input was sent.

The 26.3 source audit found that mushroom, rabbit, and beetroot stew have food components and convert to bowls on use. `Consumable.startConsuming` starts a 1.6-second use when the player can eat; at full hunger vanilla returns a failed interaction. Auto Soup still sends the ordinary item-use packet at full hunger because soup PvP plugins may handle that packet, then waits for an inventory change before retrying.

## Auto Soup

Only Auto Soup was enabled. The player had 10 health, 11 food, and one mushroom stew in hotbar slot 1. Within three seconds of the damage command, the server reported a bowl in slot 1, 17 food, 10 health, and selected slot 0. Auto Soup selected and consumed the stew, then restored stone. Vanilla stew did not directly heal health.

## One-shot Soup

With health above Auto Soup's 12-health threshold, food at 8, and mushroom stew in hotbar slot 3, pressing the bound K key toggled Soup. The server reported a bowl in slot 3, 14 food, and selected slot 0. The saved client config recorded `"soup": false` afterward, confirming that the one-shot module disabled itself. Auto Soup remained enabled but did not trigger because health was above its threshold.

These tests confirm normal 26.3 consumable use and hotbar restoration. They do not test a server plugin that turns soup into immediate health, or the optional rabbit and beetroot stew setting.
