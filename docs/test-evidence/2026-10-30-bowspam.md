# Bow Spam gameplay test — 2026-10-30

I ran the 26.3 Fabric client against a local vanilla 26.3 multiplayer server. Bow Spam was enabled; Auto Bow and Auto Bow Release were disabled. The test player was in survival mode on a level stone platform, with the weapon selected and 64 arrows in inventory. I held the use mouse button with Xdotool, then queried the server for the remaining arrow count with `clear Player496 minecraft:arrow 0`, which reports a count without removing items.

| Weapon | Held use | Charge setting | Server arrows before → after | Result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Bow | 6 seconds | 8 ticks | 64 → 49 | 15 arrows consumed across repeated shots |
| Crossbow | 7 seconds | Vanilla charge duration + 1 tick | 64 → 60 | 4 arrows consumed across repeated shots |

The server also awarded **Ol' Betsy** and **Take Aim** during the crossbow run and recorded that a pig left from the preceding setup **was shot by Player496**. The arrow counts establish that both weapon paths repeatedly fired; the pig hit is additional evidence for the crossbow path. The local server had no anticheat plugin, and this test did not measure accuracy, moving targets, or compatibility with anticheats. A shorter bow charge produces lower launch power under vanilla 26.3 rules.
