# SmartEat local gameplay test — 2026-10-30

I ran Anomoly in a local vanilla Minecraft Java 26.3 multiplayer world with SmartEat enabled and AutoEat and Auto Buff disabled. Server commands supplied items and read the player's food, health, absorption, inventory, equipment, and selected item. Automatic and manual modes were tested separately.

| Case | Server observation |
| --- | --- |
| Main inventory food choice | With stone selected, steak and bread in main inventory, and rotten flesh in the hotbar, a Hunger effect triggered eating. One steak was consumed; bread and rotten flesh were untouched. After the swap delay, stone returned to the selected hotbar slot and the remaining steak returned to main inventory. |
| Golden apple and healing potion | At 8 health with two golden apples and one drinkable Healing potion in main inventory, SmartEat consumed one apple. Absorption rose to 4, which stopped another apple from being used. The potion then raised health from 10 to 16 and left a glass bottle. One golden apple remained and stone returned to the selected slot. |
| Offhand food | With steak in the offhand and bread in main inventory, a Hunger effect lowered food enough to trigger eating. Two steaks were consumed from the offhand, food returned to 20, and stone stayed selected. |
| Unsafe food | At hunger 1, three rotten flesh and three pufferfish remained untouched. |
| Enchanted apple priority | At 2 health with an enchanted golden apple and a normal golden apple available, the enchanted apple was consumed, absorption rose to 16, and the normal apple remained. |
| Manual main-inventory food | With automatic eating disabled, food at 7, an iron pickaxe selected, a shield in the offhand, and steak in main inventory, a real held right-click consumed steak. The pickaxe stayed selected throughout, and the shield returned to the offhand after use. |
| Manual hotbar food | With automatic eating disabled, food at 0, a pickaxe selected, a shield in the offhand, and bread in hotbar slot 2, a held right-click consumed the bread through the offhand. Server queries showed food rising to 5, 10, then 15; the selected pickaxe stayed in slot 0 and the shield returned to the offhand when bread ran out. |

The first low-health run consumed two normal apples before absorption was considered. I changed the apple threshold to include existing absorption and repeated the run; the second apple remained. The automatic and manual inventory paths, food ranking, emergency priorities, and displaced-offhand restoration are gameplay verified on vanilla. A [final client screenshot](../screenshots/smarteat-preview.png) confirms the food preview renders in a native offhand slot frame beside the hotbar. Combat pause timing, custom foods, and third-party anticheats remain untested.
