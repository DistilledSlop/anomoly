# Bed Aura gameplay test — 2026-10-30

Tested with Anomoly on a local vanilla Minecraft Java 26.3 server. PlayerC stood near `(100.5,100,50.5)` on a stone floor, with a stationary 300-health pig near `(105.5,100,50.5)`. Bed Aura's `target_mobs` setting was enabled. The server console queried block states, inventory, selected item, and health. The positive survival probes used `max_self_damage=60`, `health_reserve=10`, and `min_advantage=0` so the player could survive an exposed blast; the safety probe restored the defaults of 8, 4, and 2.

| Probe | Server observation |
| --- | --- |
| No bed available | With the pig present in the Nether and no bed in inventory, candidate cells stayed air and the pig remained at 300 health. |
| Existing bed | A bed already placed in the Nether detonated without a bed item. Both halves became air, the pig fell from 300 to 240.30185 health, and PlayerC fell from 100 to 54.804497. The head was obscured from the client's view; clicking the foot completed the detonation. |
| Full Nether cycle | With one red bed in hotbar slot 1 and stone selected in slot 0, Bed Aura placed and detonated the bed. The bed item was consumed, stone remained selected, the pig fell from 300 to 240.30185, and PlayerC fell from 100 to 69.8045. |
| Full End cycle | With the same setup in the End, one bed was consumed, stone remained selected, the pig fell from 300 to 240.30185, and PlayerC fell from 100 to 79.8045. |
| Overworld guard | With permissive blast limits and one bed available, Bed Aura placed nothing in the Overworld; the pig stayed at 300 health and the bed remained in inventory. |
| Default survival safety | With the normal blast limits in the Nether, Bed Aura placed nothing at the exposed position; the pig stayed at 300 health and the bed remained in inventory. |

Minecraft 26.3 removes both bed halves and explodes at the head when a bed is used in the vanilla Nether or End. The scorer estimates the radius-five blast while treating both halves as absent during exposure sampling. Target armor and enchantment reductions, moving targets, offhand placement, protected regions, custom dimension rules, and third-party anticheats still need separate gameplay tests.
