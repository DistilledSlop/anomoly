# AirPlace gameplay test — 2026-10-30

Tested with Anomoly on a local vanilla Minecraft Java 26.3 server. A survival player stood on a stone floor, aimed horizontally into an empty arena, and right-clicked in the game window. Server commands queried blocks, entities, inventory counts, and the selected hotbar slot. The aimed block at default reach had no supporting block below it or beside it.

| Probe | Server observation |
| --- | --- |
| Floating block | One right-click put obsidian at `(704, 101, 700)` in empty air and reduced the hotbar stack from 16 to 15. |
| Spawn egg | A chicken spawn egg produced a chicken at `(704.5, 100, 700.5)` and reduced its stack from two to one. |
| Armor stand | An armor stand appeared at `(704.5, 100, 700.5)` and reduced its stack from two to one. |
| Firework rocket | A firework rocket entity appeared near the aimed air position and reduced its stack from two to one. |
| Offhand | With an iron pickaxe selected, a right-click placed offhand obsidian at the aimed air position. The selected hotbar slot stayed 0. |
| Existing block | A right-click on a stone face at `(703, 101, 700)` placed obsidian on its near face at `(702, 101, 700)`. The farther air position stayed empty. |
| Water | A right-click toward a water source at the air placement position left the water source intact. |
| Fixed range | With `custom_range=true` and `range=2`, a click placed obsidian at `(722, 101, 720)`, while the default-distance position `(724, 101, 720)` stayed empty. |

AirPlace changes only a real right-click aimed at a miss. It uses Minecraft's ordinary block-use interaction and keeps the target within the player's vanilla block reach. The local test does not cover every possible block or spawn egg, moving players, protected regions, or third-party anticheats. A three-dimensional placement preview is not yet implemented.
