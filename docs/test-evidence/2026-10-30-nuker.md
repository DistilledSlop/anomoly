# Nuker and NukerLegit gameplay test — 2026-10-30

Tested with Anomoly on a local vanilla Minecraft Java 26.3 server. A stationary survival player stood at `(300.5,100,300.5)` on a stone platform with a dirt block underfoot. The player held a wooden sword in hotbar slot 0 and had an iron shovel in slot 1. Both modules were configured to mine only `minecraft:dirt`; their other safety settings remained on. The server console queried block states, player rotation, inventory, and selected slot.

| Probe | Server observation |
| --- | --- |
| Nuker three-block run | With Nuker enabled, dirt at `(302,100,300)`, `(303,100,300)`, and `(304,100,300)` became air without a manual attack. The iron shovel took exactly three durability points, and the wooden sword in slot 0 was selected again afterward. |
| Floor and filter controls | Dirt directly under the player at `(300,99,300)` remained, as did a stone block at `(303,100,301)` excluded by the dirt-only filter. |
| NukerLegit blocked view | With Nuker disabled and NukerLegit enabled, a stone block at `(302,100,300)` blocked the view of dirt at `(303,100,300)`. The dirt remained and the sword stayed selected. |
| NukerLegit clear view | Removing the intervening stone let NukerLegit turn down toward the dirt and mine it. The shovel's damage rose from 3 to 4 and the sword was reselected. |
| NukerLegit field of view | A dirt block behind the player at `(298,100,300)` remained while the player faced east. After the server turned the player west, it was mined, shovel damage rose to 5, and the sword was reselected. |
| Actual-reach cap | With the rebuilt client, dirt at `(302,100,300)` was mined and shovel damage rose to 6. Dirt at `(305,100,300)`, beyond the player's 4.5-block survival interaction reach, remained intact even though Nuker's configured scan radius was five blocks. The sword was reselected. |

Both modules use the ordinary client mining start, continue, and stop actions. NukerLegit additionally requires a visible target and confirms that the crosshair points to the target face before mining. The client caps its scan at the player's actual block interaction reach. Vanilla block-break crack rendering shows progress. Creative mining, protected regions, moving players, mixed block types, and third-party anticheats still need separate gameplay tests. “Legit” describes the interaction path; it does not guarantee anticheat acceptance.
