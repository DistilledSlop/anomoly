# Parkour local gameplay test — 2026-10-30

I ran Anomoly against a local vanilla Minecraft Java 26.3 server. A single stone block at (80, 120, 80) formed the starting platform, with air immediately ahead. The player started at (80.5, 121, 80.5), faced south, and held W. I queried the server-side position during each run. Auto Jump, Air Jump, Auto Walk, Safe Walk, and other movement modules were disabled.

| Parkour state | Server position samples (X, Y, Z) |
| --- | --- |
| Off | (80.5, 121.00, 81.13), (80.5, 118.31, 82.62), (80.5, 106.45, 84.91) |
| On | (80.5, 121.00, 80.75), (80.5, 122.02, 82.62), (80.5, 118.22, 84.11) |

With Parkour off, walking past the edge led to a fall. With it on, the server saw a jump above the platform as the player passed the edge. The different sample times and distances make these position samples behavioral evidence, not a timed speed comparison.

I then placed a stone landing path from z=82 through z=86 and repeated the enabled run. After walking across the one-block gap, the server reported (80.5, 121.00, 83.47) and then (80.5, 121.00, 83.49), showing a stable landing. On the continuous path, a shorter W hold moved the player from z=83.50 to z=84.57 while Y stayed at 121.00 across three samples. Sneaking, other jump modules, and third-party anticheats were not tested.
