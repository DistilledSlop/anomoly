# Eagle local gameplay test — 2026-10-30

I ran Anomoly's Fabric 26.3 client against a local vanilla 26.3 server. A single stone block at `(50,119,50)` formed an isolated platform. I used `minecraft.custom:minecraft.sneak_time` as a server observed measure of crouching, and queried player position and health after movement.

I set Eagle's minimum and maximum edge distance to `0.2` for a deterministic test. At the center of the platform, `(50.5,120,50.5)`, the player had no crouch score. At `(50.83,120,50.5)` near the unsupported east edge, the score rose from 43 to 108. Moving back to the center left it at 110 after the transition, and it remained 110 in the later query.

With Eagle enabled, I teleported a second player to the center facing east and held forward for two seconds. The server measured `(51.29739931090721,120,50.5)`, health 20, and a crouch score of 66. The player remained on the one-block platform; the position is just before the hitbox loses support at about x=51.3. With Offhand Auto enabled at the same time, putting a shield in the offhand and 16 stone in inventory caused the server to show the 16-stone stack in the offhand and the shield in the vacated inventory slot.

The first implementation changed the sneak key during the module tick, which did not reach the server. The second implementation crouched while stationary but released once the player's center crossed the edge; a two-second walk fell to the lower terrain and left 3 health. I moved the decision into `KeyboardInput.tick` and measured support under the player's full hitbox. The final walk above passed. Direction, held-block, and pitch conditions, unusual collision shapes, and third-party anticheats remain untested.
