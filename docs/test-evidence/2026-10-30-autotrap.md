# Auto Trap gameplay test — 2026-10-30

Tested with Anomoly on a local vanilla Minecraft Java 26.3 server. PlayerA stood at `(30.5,101,30.5)` on a one-block-high stone platform, with stone selected in hotbar slot 0 and the mode's material in slot 1. A stationary pig stood at `(32.5,100,30.5)` on a stone floor. `target_mobs=true` enabled this controlled mob target; the module normally targets players. The default line-of-sight check was enabled, and the test place range was 5 blocks.

| Mode | Server observation |
| --- | --- |
| `box` | Obsidian filled all four side positions at Y 100, Y 101, and Y 102 around `(32,100,30)`, plus the roof at `(32,102,30)`. The selected item remained stone. An initial build with only two wall levels placed eight sides but could not attach the roof; adding the upper support ring resolved this. |
| No target | After killing the target and removing the west lower wall, the gap stayed air. |
| `web` | Cobweb appeared at `(32,100,30)` while the pig occupied the block. Stone remained selected. |
| `ignite` | Fire appeared at `(32,100,30)` with the pig still present at the first check. Stone remained selected. |
| `lava` | A lava source appeared at `(32,100,30)` after the client turned toward the floor, waited one client tick, and used the bucket. Stone remained selected. |

All four modes used normal client interactions. Box, web, and ignite used `MultiPlayerGameMode.useItemOn`; lava used `useItem` after a view rotation because Minecraft 26.3 buckets raycast from the player's view. No kick or disconnect occurred. This verifies stationary-mob placement in creative mode on a vanilla server. Player targets, offhand materials, alternate box blocks, moving targets, protected regions, and third-party anticheat compatibility remain unverified.
